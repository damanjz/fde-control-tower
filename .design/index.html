Tarangini Control Tower: three concepts

Same data, three different products. The network (4 warehouses, 36 cities, 40 suppliers, 3 carriers, 200 products) is the real Supply Chain project data; the morning's trucks, incidents and money at risk are simulated. Pick a direction; the look, the layout and the one signature interaction come as a set.

A · Control RoomMap-first and dark. Every truck moves on its lane; pick an incident and the map flies to it. Role switch, approvals, outage switch. B · Triage DeskQueue-first, light and editorial. Each incident reads as a brief you can act on; switch who you are and the desk redraws under that role. C · RippleGraph-first and cinematic. An incident ripples from its source to the customers it reaches; press play to replay the morning.
